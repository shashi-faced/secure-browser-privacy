# Secure Browser Privacy Policy

App: Secure Browser, package com.safebrowser, on Google Play

Developer: Utils Container, Shashi Kumar Verma

Contact: shashikumarverma23@gmail.com

Last updated: 31 October 2026

## 1. Summary

Secure Browser is a web browser for Android. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal or sensitive user data. We do not run any servers that receive your data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## 2. Data the app keeps on your device only

Bookmarks and open tabs are kept only on your device. Files you choose to download are saved to your device storage. Incognito tabs do not keep history, cookies or site data after they are closed. None of this data is sent to us or to any third party by the app.

## 3. Network connections

Web pages: when you open a website, your device connects directly to that website, as in any browser. That website receives the information browsers normally send, such as your IP address and browser details, and is governed by its own privacy policy.

Search: text you type that is not a web address is sent to DuckDuckGo at duckduckgo.com to show search results. DuckDuckGo's privacy policy applies.

Home page and connection check: the app opens www.google.com as its home page and contacts www.google.com to check whether you are online before a download. No personal data is included in this check.

Web pages are shown using Android System WebView, provided by Google as part of Android.

## 4. Permissions

Internet: needed to load web pages.

Storage, on Android 12 and below only: used only when you choose to download a file, to save it to your device.

## 5. Data sharing and selling

We do not share or sell user data with anyone.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. You can delete bookmarks inside the app, delete downloaded files with your file manager, or remove all app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## 8. Security

Data stays on your device and is protected by the Android app sandbox. The app warns you when a site uses an insecure HTTP connection.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new Last updated date.
